=================================================================
      SAR OIL SPILL DETECTION — OTSU BASELINE EVALUATION
=================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Otsu's thresholding was evaluated on the held-out test set (1,615 samples)
as a classical heuristic baseline. Because oil spills appear dark in SAR imagery,
Otsu automatically calculates the intra-class variance minimizing threshold.

EVALUATION METRICS SUMMARY
--------------------------
Accuracy         : 60.33%
Precision        : 37.85%
Recall           : 80.92%
Specificity      : 53.05%
F1 Score (Dice)  : 51.58%
IoU (Jaccard)    : 34.75%
ROC-AUC          : 84.09%
Avg Precision    : 67.03%

WHY DEEP LEARNING (SegNet / U-Net) IS SUPERIOR:
-----------------------------------------------
1. Otsu achieves reasonable recall but suffers from massive False Positives (low precision),
   because it cannot distinguish real oil spills from SAR 'look-alikes'
   (calm water, low-wind zones, natural slicks, and land shadows).
2. SegNet and U-Net learn spatial shapes, continuous slick geometries,
   and texture contexts that filter out look-alikes effectively.
